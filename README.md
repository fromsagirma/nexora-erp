# Nexora ERP

### Inventory, Sales and Purchasing Management MVP

An individual full-stack ERP project developed as part of the
Nexora Technologies internship program.

## Project Overview

Nexora ERP is a web-based inventory, sales, and purchasing
management MVP designed to help businesses manage products,
warehouses, stock movements, customers, suppliers, sales orders,
and purchase orders through a centralized system.

## Project Objectives

- Manage products, categories, and warehouses.
- Monitor inventory levels and stock movements.
- Manage customers and sales orders.
- Manage suppliers and purchase orders.
- Support partial and complete purchase receiving.
- Prevent invalid stock operations.
- Maintain an auditable history of sensitive operations.
- Provide role-based access to system features.
